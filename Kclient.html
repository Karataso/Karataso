What it is

Kclient is a ghost client: instead of blatant exploits, its combat modules are tuned to mimic human behavior — humanized click patterns, reaction-based aim, tick-accurate resets — so the edge stays within what anti-cheats tolerate rather than fighting them head-on.

There is no telemetry, no phoning home, no account system. Everything runs locally and your configs stay on your disk. The full source is published on Codeberg and can be audited or built yourself.

Modules

Two ClickGUIs ship with the client: cheat modules (blue accent) and legit modules (green accent). A few examples per area — the full breakdown (~60 modules) lives in FEATURES.md.

Combat

AutoClicker with selectable click patterns, Aim Assist, Reach, HitSelect, BlockHit with a predictive mode, Backtrack.

Movement

BetterMove (SnapTap + Instant Stop), Sprint Reset (W-Tap / S-Tap), Jump Reset, KB Delay, Sprint.

Latency tools

Backtrack with queue-based packet release, Fake Lag (exit-only desync), Blink, Ping Fix so lag modules don't spike your scoreboard ping.

Visuals & HUD

ESP, Tracers, Nametags, Xray, Chams, BedESP, plus a HUD editor: keystrokes, armor, effects, reach display, and more.

Legit side

1.7-style animations and item positions, camera tweaks with FreeLook, No Hurt Cam, Fullbright — the above-board half of the client.

Client

Terminal with bash-like history, chat commands (. prefix), profile manager with favorites, scripts manager, custom themes with gradient pickers.

Getting it

  1. Install Forge 1.8.9.
  2. Grab keystrokes.jar from the latest release and drop it into your .minecraft/mods folder.
  3. Launch the game and open the ClickGUI with your configured keybind. Chat commands use the . prefix.

Building from source: JDK 8 for the project, Java 17 for the Gradle JVM, then ./gradlew build. See the repo README.
